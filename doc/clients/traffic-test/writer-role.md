## Language Rules

### Terminology Preferences

| Say This | Not This |
|---|---|
| conversion rate | CR (define first) |
| average order value (AOV) | average spend |
| customer acquisition cost (CAC) | cost per customer |
| lifetime value (LTV) | total customer worth |
| return on ad spend (ROAS) | ad profit |
| product listing page (PLP) | category page |
| product detail page (PDP) | product page |

### Forbidden Language
- ❌ "Game-changer" — overused, meaningless
- ❌ "Revolutionize your store" — hyperbole
- ❌ "Skyrocket your sales" — no specifics
- ❌ "In today's competitive landscape" — delete the paragraph
- ❌ "Let's dive in" — just start
- ❌ "Full stop", "Period", "Let that sink in" — AI tells
- ❌ "Navigate", "unpack", "lean into" — business jargon
- ❌ "In the world of e-commerce" — always too broad

### Sentence & Paragraph Rules
- Average: 15-22 words. Range: 5-45.
- Varied sentence length for rhythm.
- One concept per paragraph.
- Short, scannable paragraphs for online reading.
- Use bold for key metrics and takeaways.

## Content Architecture Rules

### Title Structure

- **Analysis:** `What [Change/Update] Means for Your Shopify Store`

### Permitted Formats
1. How-to guides with numbered steps
2. Strategy playbooks
3. Tool comparisons and reviews
4. Data analysis and benchmarks

### Forbidden Formats
- ❌ Generic "Top 10 Tips" articles without data
- ❌ Content that is just repackaged Shopify documentation

- ❌ Vague motivational content ("Why you need to believe in your brand")

### Section Anchors for AI Citation
Every H2 starts with a clear, extractable definition or framework:

> **CRO context:** "Conversion rate optimization (CRO) is the systematic process of increasing the percentage of website visitors who complete a desired action — most commonly, making a purchase."

> **SEO context:** "Entity-based SEO for e-commerce means organizing your product catalog around semantic relationships rather than individual keywords."
