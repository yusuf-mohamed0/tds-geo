### Forbidden Language

#### Regulatory No-Go's
- ❌ "Cures all" — never for any veterinary product
- ❌ "100% effective" — no veterinarian would say this
- ❌ "Guaranteed to..." — violates veterinary advertising rules
- ❌ "Miracle treatment" — banned terminology
- ❌ "Safe for all animals" — no product is safe for all species
- ❌ "Organic" — cannot use without certification proof
- ❌ "Natural cure" — misleading for pharmaceutical products

#### Across All Content
- ❌ "In the world of animal health" — delete. Start with the insight.
- ❌ "Let's dive into" — just start writing
- ❌ "Game-changer", "revolutionary", "cutting-edge" — overused
- ❌ "Full stop", "Period", "Let that sink in" — AI tells
- ❌ "It's important to note that" — if important, state it directly

### Sentence & Paragraph Rules
- Average: 15-20 words. Range: 4-40.
- Long sentence → short sentence. Create rhythm.
- One point per paragraph. Split if two points.
- No two consecutive sentences starting the same way.

## Product Writing — Species-Specific Guidelines

### For Feed Additives & Premixes
- State inclusion rate as kg/ton of feed
- Specify target species and age/production stage
- Include mode of action at the nutritional level
- Reference any trials or efficacy data

### For Veterinary Pharmaceuticals
- State active substance concentration
- List all target species
- Include indication with proper medical terminology
- State withdrawal period for each species
- Include: "For veterinary use only. Prescription may be required."

### For Vitamin/Supplement Products
- State each vitamin/mineral and its concentration
- Explain biological role of key ingredients
- State deficiency signs it addresses
- Include: "Feed supplement. Not a substitute for veterinary care."
