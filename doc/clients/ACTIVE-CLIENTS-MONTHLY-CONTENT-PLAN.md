# Active Clients Monthly Content Plan

Last updated: 2026-08-13

Scope: active clients only — `alamein-2022`, `boston-pharma`, `caravanserai`, and `flaunt-cosmetics-global`.

Inactive, historical, or pending clients are excluded: `traffic-test`, `boston-vet`, `joes-venture`, `acme-maintenance`.

## Non-Negotiable Workflow

Every article must be written from the client's own operating documents before drafting:

- `brand-profile.md` for brand identity, visual language, audience, values, products, and differentiators.
- `writer-role.md` for voice, forbidden phrases, role-specific expertise, tone, and compliance boundaries.
- `seo-content-guide.md` for content pillars, target topics, keyword categories, and gaps.
- `geo-aeo-plan.md` for answer-first structure, entity positioning, and AI-search-ready sections.
- `CONTENT-OPERATING-ROLES.md` and `EEAT-content-framework.md` for shared quality gates.

Local drafts may include internal QA blocks. Shopify or WordPress bodies must not. Public CMS bodies must contain only customer-facing content, SEO metadata, excerpt/summary, approved tags, and platform-safe formatting.

## Monthly Publishing Rules

- Produce 4 local drafts per active client each month.
- Keep every article `status: local_draft` until approval and connector verification.
- Shopify drafts must remain hidden first: `published: false`, `published_at: null`.
- WordPress posts must remain draft or pending review until write credentials and approval are confirmed.
- No scheduling or live publishing without explicit approval.
- Each article must include clear SEO title, meta description, excerpt, primary keyword, target audience, customer-facing CTA, and CMS-safe body.

## QA Before Any CMS Upload

Every upload attempt must answer yes to all checks:

- Does the article sound like this exact client, not a generic article?
- Does it use the client's product lines, market, geography, and constraints naturally?
- Does it obey all `writer-role.md` forbidden-language rules?
- Is the body free of internal planning labels and publishing checklists?
- Are SEO title, meta description, excerpt, tags, and slug customer-facing?
- Is the connector verified and safe for draft creation?
- Is approval explicit for upload or scheduling?

If any answer is no, do not upload.
