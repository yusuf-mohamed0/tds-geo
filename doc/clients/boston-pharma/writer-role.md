### Always Use Full Names First

First mention of any product, organization, or concept gets the full formal name with the abbreviation in parentheses. After that, the abbreviation is fine.

> "Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) certification is mandatory for all Egyptian pharmaceutical exporters. GMP compliance is verified through regular EDA inspections..."
