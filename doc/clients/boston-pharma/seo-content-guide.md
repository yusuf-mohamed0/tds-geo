## Keyword Strategy
- Primary: Egyptian pharmaceutical company, pharmaceutical manufacturer Egypt
- Secondary: dietary supplements Egypt, vitamins Egypt, generic medicines
- Long-tail: "quality pharmaceutical products Egypt", "GMP certified pharma Egypt"
- Arabic equivalents (Weglot translates automatically)

## Brand Voice Guidelines
- **Tone**: Professional, trustworthy, scientifically grounded
- **Language**: English (primary), Arabic (secondary via Weglot)
- **Style**: Patient-centric, educational, transparent about ingredients/science
- **Key phrases**: "safe and effective", "premium quality raw materials", "real science", "certified guaranteed"
- **Target reader**: Healthcare professionals and informed consumers

## Content Pillars
1. **Regulatory & Compliance** — GMP, FDA/EMA standards, Egyptian pharma regulations
2. **Product Education** — Supplement guides, vitamin benefits, dosage info
3. **Company Authority** — Manufacturing campus, R&D investment, quality processes
4. **Industry Insights** — Egyptian pharma market, export opportunities, healthcare trends
5. **Patient Wellness** — Nutrition advice, preventive care, treatment adherence

## EEAT Considerations
- Cite Egyptian pharmaceutical regulations (EDA)
- Reference GMP certifications
- Mention the 10th of Ramadan City manufacturing campus
- Quote company mission/values where relevant
- Avoid claims that violate Egyptian pharma advertising regulations
- Use the brand voice "Boston Pharmaceutical Industries" (not just "Boston Pharma" for formal content)
