## Darb Console
**URL:** https://caravanserai-design.com/products/darb-console

In ancient Egypt, the word *darb* signified a path or gateway — and this console table ushers a sense of arrival into any room. Hand-carved from solid mango wood by Egyptian artisans, each piece tells a story through its intricate geometric motifs. The warm wood grain and hand-rubbed finish make it both striking and versatile. Use it in an entryway to display brass trays and a dried palm arrangement, or as a statement piece behind a deep sofa. Dust with a soft, dry cloth; keep away from direct sunlight to preserve the natural patina. Let the Darb Console be the threshold to your home's soul.

**Category:** Console Tables
**Styling Tip:** Layer a round mirror above and place a ceramic vase with dried eucalyptus on top for a balanced, sculptural entry vignette.
**Care:** Wipe with a dry microfiber cloth; avoid harsh chemicals and prolonged exposure to direct sunlight or humidity.

## Fatima's Hand Naqsha
**URL:** https://caravanserai-design.com/products/fatimas-hand-naqsha

The Hand of Fatima has been a symbol of protection across the Middle East for centuries — now it guards your walls with beauty and purpose. This *naqsha* (wall hook) is cast in solid brass by Egyptian metalworkers and given an aged antique finish that deepens over time. Five delicately curved fingers hold your scarves, keys, or lightweight bags while the pointed thumb serves as an elegant focal hook. Install it beside the entry mirror or in a powder room for an instant cultural conversation piece. Polish occasionally with a brass-safe cream; avoid abrasive pads. Welcome blessing into your daily routine — one hook at a time.

**Category:** Wall Decor / Hooks
**Styling Tip:** Hang three at staggered heights along a hallway to create a functional art installation against a white or ochre wall.
**Care:** Dust regularly; use a soft brass polish every few months to maintain the antique finish — never use abrasive cleaners.
