## Mission

> To elevate home decor experiences by offering a carefully curated selection of artisanal accessories that blend timeless elegance with contemporary flair.

## Vision

> To be the prime destination for those who appreciate the art of home decoration and to become a leading presence in the gift-giving industry.

## Brand Philosophy

Caravanserai was born from a simple belief: the spaces we live in quietly shape the way we live. Not through grand gestures, but through the details we touch every day — the curve of a chair's arm, the weight of a wooden table, the way light settles across a carved surface by afternoon.
