## Brand Story

Founded in 1987 as a family business. Hamid Sabry, an artist and mechanical engineer, began making classic furniture in the 1980s, then built billiard tables (pioneering Egypt's first billiard hall). In the early 1990s, inspired by his own children, he identified an underserved niche: children's playgrounds. Today Alamein continues to develop jungle gyms, swing sets, playhouses, seesaws and more.

Later Hamid designed a line of batch-producible outdoor furniture — chairs, tables, benches, umbrellas — all foldable, stackable, and easy to store. The portfolio now includes outdoor & street furniture, play systems (game tables, playgrounds, play equipment), and outdoor fitness equipment.
