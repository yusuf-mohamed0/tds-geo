# Flaunt Cosmetics Global Audit — July 2026

## Content-Length Incident

### Finding
Four scheduled articles created on July 12 contained one-sentence placeholders despite stored `word_count` values of 350–450. Their actual bodies were 64–81 characters long and did not meet the required article depth.

### Initial Remediation

- Removed all four records from the publishing schedule and changed their status to `rejected` before any could publish.
- Regenerated the records through the production content service as unscheduled `generated` drafts.

### Editorial-Safety Follow-up

The first regenerated versions met the length requirement but failed editorial review. They contained raw HTML treated as Markdown, invented first-person experience, unsupported statistics, and unapproved product names. None were visible or published.

- Replaced all four existing hidden Shopify drafts with fact-constrained Markdown sources and regenerated valid HTML.
- Verified stored word counts: 1,917, 1,770, 1,909, and 2,191 words.
- Confirmed all four remain `generated`, have no `scheduled_at` or `published_at` value, and are hidden in Shopify.
- Set Flaunt Cosmetics Global to manual approval mode.
- Moved all four corrected hidden drafts from News to Beauty Tips (`116005503268`) and deleted the unpublished News copies.

### Permanent Controls

- Minimum article length is 1,200 actual content words; client configuration may increase but not lower this floor.
- The generator rejects short model output.
- Article saving, approval, scheduling, manual publishing, embedded publishing, and auto-publishing independently enforce the minimum.
- Auto-publish rejects and unschedules any legacy article that does not meet the requirement.
- Generation now requires Markdown rather than raw HTML; document-level tags and HTML comments are rejected.
- Generation and publishing reject fabricated first-party experience, unsupported statistics, and unapproved Flaunt product names.
- The central publisher repeats the safety check, so no route can bypass it.
- Beauty Tips is stored as the client and Shopify connector default; all publishing paths use it instead of selecting the first Shopify blog.

### Next Action

Review the four corrected hidden drafts, then explicitly approve and schedule them for the News blog.
