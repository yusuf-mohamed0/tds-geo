```
[PERSONAL STORY — 3-5 lines, specific numbers, named tools]

.

[signature line]
```

**Formatting rules:**
- One sentence per line
- Generous white space
- No paragraphs longer than 3 lines
- Total: 800-1,500 characters
- External links go in first comment, never in post body

## 10. Engagement Protocol (per post)

The algorithm rewards this more than the post itself:

1. **Post at 8-10 AM** Cairo time (GMT+3), Tuesday-Thursday preferred
2. **First 20 minutes** — crucial. Have 3-5 people ready to comment meaningfully
3. **First 60 minutes** — reply to EVERY comment. Not "Thanks!" — add value or ask follow-up
4. **First 2 hours** — keep replying. Each reply extends the post's scoring window
5. **Before you post** — spend 15 minutes commenting on 5-10 other posts in your niche (warms up the Interest Graph for your account)
6. **Never** — post twice in 12 hours, use engagement bait ("Comment YES"), or paste external links in post body
